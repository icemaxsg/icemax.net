iceMax SG Pte. Ltd. is a Singapore-based technology infrastructure provider specialising
in edge network security and managed multi-cloud deployment.

We eliminate technical friction between edge security layers and origin server
environments. By leveraging Cloudflare's global network as our primary defence
architecture alongside targeted AWS and GCP deployments, we enable enterprise clients to
maintain uptime, lower latency, and control IT operating costs.

Our engagements are structured around measurable operational outcomes: what reaches the
origin, how quickly it is served, and what the supporting infrastructure costs to run.
