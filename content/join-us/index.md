At iceMax SG Pte. Ltd., we focus on perimeter defence, cloud infrastructure stability,
and multi-cloud network routing. Operating from Singapore's technology hub, our team
solves network performance and security challenges for enterprise clients across
international markets.

If you possess deep technical competence in edge defence or cloud computing and value
precise engineering execution, we invite you to connect with us.

We evaluate technical candidates on an ongoing basis. Send your resume and technical
summary directly to our talent acquisition team at
[careers@icemax.net](mailto:careers@icemax.net). Please specify your primary technical
focus — Edge Security, Cloud Infrastructure, or Enterprise Systems — in the subject line.
